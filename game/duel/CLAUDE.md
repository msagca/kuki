# The duel

A one-on-one fight over the network (`NetworkSystem`: `--host`, `--join`, and `--lag`/`--jitter`/
`--loss` to stand one PC in for the internet). It uses everything in the root `CLAUDE.md`'s "Fixed
tick and input": the tick, gestures, the stepped animator and inverse kinematics.

The duel opens on a select screen (`DuelSystem`'s `Screen`): A/D choose, Enter fights, Escape backs
out a step at a time. A `--capture` taken without anyone at the keyboard shows that screen.

Its roster of four fighters is generated by `tools/make_fighter.py` (one rig, clips made once and
shared by every armature, so any two can fight), and an armature in a .blend's `Export` collection
is exported with its skin and clips. Its four stages are generated by `tools/make_stages.py` into
`stages.blend` (one object a part and material, built in place) and dressed in `stage.cpp`;
`StageSystem` builds the one `--stage` names (alley, temple, rooftop, shrine), switches on Tab and
animates their lights and lightning. Both scripts are run by hand and their .blend files committed.

The rules (`duel_sim.cpp`) are windowless and compiled into the tests as well.
